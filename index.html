          About the reference ranges
          
      Ranges are typical values for healthy adults from published guidelines. A chest strap is a single, non-standard ECG lead, so values outside a range are a prompt to look closer, not a diagnosis. Heart-rate and HRV ranges apply to resting recordings only and adjust to the age and sex in Settings.

        For a good overnight recording
        
        
          
          
        • Wet the strap electrodes and wear the strap snugly with the Polar logo upright.
          • 
          
        • Keep the phone next to the bed, plugged into a charger, with this page open. The screen stays on; use Dim screen to darken it.

          About these results
          
            Asleep/awake, breathing, heart rate and HRV are measured from the strap. Sleep stages (deep, light, REM) are estimated from heart rate, HRV, breathing regularity and stillness; such estimates typically agree with a laboratory sleep study about 60–75 % of the time, so treat them as a guide. Blood oxygen (SpO₂) isn't available because the Polar H10 has no optical sensor.

            How to measure your waist
            
                
              
              1. Stand up straight with your feet together and your stomach relaxed. Measure against bare skin or thin clothing.
                2. 
              
              2. Find the bottom of your lowest rib and the top of your hip bone at your side.
                3. 
              
              3. Wrap a flexible tape measure around your body halfway between those two points. For most people this is roughly level with the belly button.
                4. 
              
              4. Keep the tape level all the way round, snug but not pressing into the skin.
                5. 
              
              5. Breathe out normally and read the measurement at the end of the breath, without pulling your stomach in.
                6. 
              
              6. Measure twice; if the two readings differ by more than 1 cm, measure again and use the average.
                7.
